About Us

Serious Training. Serious Results.

Lorenzo's Dog Training Team helps dogs of any age, size, breed, and temperament become more reliable in real life. The mission is practical: keep dogs out of shelters and in safe, happy homes by teaching owners leadership, rules, boundaries, and clear communication.

  • Dog obedience training and advanced off-leash obedience.
  • Dog behavior modification for difficult or unsafe habits.
  • Specialty, service, assistance, protection, scent, utility, and retrieval training.
  • Trainer education through an Academy / Certification path.

The Technique Behind Our Training.

Lorenzo's system is built on technique, timing, communication, leadership, rules, and boundaries. The goal is not a quick trick. The goal is a dog that understands what is expected and an owner who knows how to lead with consistency.

1. Schedule an Evaluation

Start with a professional evaluation in person or by Zoom so the trainer can understand your dog, your household, and your goals.

2. Read the Dog and the Owner

We look at temperament, obedience level, behavior patterns, timing, handling, and the structure already in the home.

3. Build the Tailored Path

Your trainer recommends the right path for your pet: obedience, behavior modification, specialty training, or advanced work.

4. Train for Real Life

The program teaches the dog and the owner together so the results hold up at home, around distractions, and in daily life.

About the Founder

Lorenzo Miller

Lorenzo Miller's journey with dogs began as a child in the inner city, where he developed deep compassion for stray dogs and an early ability to calm and understand them. By age ten, his parents recognized that his interest was more than a phase and connected him with professional dog trainers.

Through the 1970s and 1980s, Lorenzo studied obedience, behavior, communication, structure, and multiple professional training methods. He paid his way through college with in-home dog training and refined a distinct approach built on clarity, accountability, timing, leadership, and transformation between dog and owner.

In 1987, Lorenzo incorporated Lorenzo's Dog Training Team. Since then, he has trained thousands of dogs and developed trainers who carry the same mission: serious training, serious results, and practical help for families who want safe, reliable, happy dogs.
