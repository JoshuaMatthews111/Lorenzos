Custom domains with simple DNS instructions.

Trainer enters their domain. The system creates records they can paste into GoDaddy, Squarespace, Cloudflare, Wix, or another DNS provider.

DNS records for trainer domain

TXT_lorenzo-verify.example.com
CNAMEwww → trainer-sites.lorenzosdogtrainingteam.com
ALIASexample.com → platform target

After verification, the domain becomes active and serves that trainer’s landing page.
