# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective dog trainer candidates in city ad markets who love dogs, communicate well with people, and want to start or grow a dog training business with Lorenzo's Dog Training Team behind them.

## Product Purpose

Convert qualified recruiting ad traffic into trainer interest leads for Lorenzo's Dog Training Team. The recruiting pages should make the opportunity feel like a serious business path, not a casual job listing, while preserving the existing lead pipeline and reporting behavior.

## Brand Personality

Serious, disciplined, opportunity-driven.

## Anti-references

Generic franchise pages, cheap pet-business ads, beige career pages, overdesigned AI landing pages, weak stock-photo recruiting pages, and public copy that describes the opportunity with trust-damaging terms.

## Design Principles

1. Make the opportunity feel serious and selective.
2. Make candidates want to build a dog training business, not simply apply for a job.
3. Keep the CTA clear and immediate.
4. Use real Lorenzo assets and proof instead of generic recruiting visuals.
5. Preserve forms, reporting, backoffice, Supabase, staff portal, ad tracking, lead reporting, and deployment behavior.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use clear contrast, readable forms, keyboard-visible focus states, and reduced-motion support. Mobile visitors must be able to understand the opportunity and submit interest without visual overlap or cramped form controls.
