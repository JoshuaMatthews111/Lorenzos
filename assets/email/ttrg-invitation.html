Thank you for being a valued client of Lorenzo’s Dog Training Team. We truly appreciate the trust you have placed in us to help you and your dog build a stronger relationship through professional training, education, and guidance.

At Lorenzo’s Dog Training Team, our mission has always been about more than simply training dogs. We believe every dog deserves the opportunity to become a well-trained, confident, and valued companion, and every individual and family should have access to the resources and support they need to succeed with their dog.

With that mission in mind, I’d like to take a moment to introduce you to our nonprofit organization, Team Trainers Rescue Group (TTRG).

Team Trainers Rescue Group was created to expand the impact of our work beyond traditional dog training. TTRG is committed to making professional dog training more accessible to individuals and families with limited financial resources while also creating more readily available access to trained service dogs for people with disabilities.

We also rescue dogs from shelters, abandonment, and homeless situations. Each dog is carefully evaluated for sound temperament, health, behavior, and overall suitability before entering our training and placement program.

Dogs who qualify receive professional training and preparation to become dependable companions, service dogs, or valuable support animals for the people who need them most.

Your support helps provide:
  • Veterinary care
  • Food and housing
  • Professional evaluations
  • Professional training and equipment
  • Service-dog preparation
  • Placement and ongoing support

Every contribution helps give a deserving dog a second chance and a meaningful purpose, while also creating life-changing support for an individual or family in need.

Thank you for being part of the Lorenzo’s Dog Training Team family and for helping us extend our impact even further through Team Trainers Rescue Group.
