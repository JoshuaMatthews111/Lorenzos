Privacy

Privacy Policy

Lorenzo's Dog Training Team collects information you choose to provide through website forms, phone calls, email, and related communications so the office can respond to your request and provide dog training information.

Information We Collect

We may collect your name, email address, phone number, address or service area, dog training needs, referral source, form submission details, and website attribution such as page URL or campaign source.

How We Use Information

We use information to respond to inquiries, schedule consultations, route requests to the appropriate office or trainer, provide dog training information, improve website performance, and maintain lead and client records.

SMS Privacy

If you voluntarily opt in to SMS messages, your mobile number and SMS consent record will be used to send promotional and informational messages from Lorenzo's Dog Training Team about dog training, consultation scheduling, follow-up, and offers.

We do not sell or share mobile numbers, SMS opt-in data, or SMS consent records with third parties for their own marketing or promotional purposes. SMS consent is optional and is not required to submit a form or purchase services.

Vendors And Service Providers

We may use trusted service providers to host the website, store form submissions, send emails or text messages, manage analytics, and support business operations. These providers are used only to help Lorenzo's Dog Training Team operate and communicate with prospects and clients.

Your Choices

You can opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help. You can also contact Lorenzo's Dog Training Team at (866) 436-4959.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be directed to Lorenzo's Dog Training Team at (866) 436-4959 or through the contact form.
