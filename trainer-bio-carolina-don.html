About Carolina Don

Carolina Don SAN ANTONIO, TEXAS Carolina was born and raised in San Diego, California and currently resides in Texas. Between her love for working with animals and her experience with the public in sales and being a personal trainer (for humans), it was only natural that she would eventually merge the two to become a dog trainer with LDTT. “I wanted to become a dog trainer because I truly love working with animals.

And I think it’s amazing to see the work that’s put in when you see the dog you trained, working. I wanted to be a part of LDTT because I want to work with people who love animals and love helping animals as much as I do.” Her goal is simply to help dogs and their families live together happily. When Carolina isn’t working on that goal, she enjoys going for walks, hiking, working out, going to the beach, and spending time with her family.
