About Jasmine Bland

Jasmine Bland Hammond, Indiana Jasmine Bland is originally from Illinois but now resides in Indiana. After seeing dog training classes going on, she knew she could do better than that, so she began a quest to find a balanced dog training program. She eventually came across Lorenzo’s and was eager to jump right in and begin her career.

With two years of veterinarian technology school under her belt, Jasmine pulls from her education to better serve her dog training clients. Her goals are simple, she’d like to take care of her mom and buy a new car. When she’s not training, she enjoys reading and studying.

To decompress after a long day, Jasmine listens to music and plays video games.
