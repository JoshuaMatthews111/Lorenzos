FINAL BUILD RULE
The coder should build every automation using:
Trigger → Condition → Message → Status → Owner → Deadline → Escalation → Exit
Example:
Trigger: Customer books evaluation Condition: Valid trainer assigned Message: Confirmation SMS Status: BOOKED Owner: Assigned trainer Deadline: Contact customer same day Escalation: Market leader if missed Exit: Trainer marks CONTACTED
That framework should be used for every pathway.

The one sentence both sides need to agree on
Executive version:
No qualified paid lead should be lost because LDTT was too slow, forgot to follow up, failed to route them correctly, or failed to recover them.
Coder version:
Every lead must always have a valid state, owner, next action, deadline, recovery path, and terminal condition.
Same mission. Different language.
That is the version I would put at the top of the coder’s build document and the executive operating plan.
