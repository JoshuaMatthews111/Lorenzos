About Tabatha Shelley

Tabatha Shelley is dedicated to helping dogs and their owners build stronger relationships through effective communication, trust, and reliable training. Born and raised in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, and now living in Panama City, Florida, Tabatha has always had a deep appreciation for dogs and the joy they bring to people's lives. Her journey with Lorenzo's Dog Training Team began after attending a Lunch & Learn presentation hosted by Victoria and Daniel at her workplace.

Seeing firsthand the impact that professional training could have on both dogs and their owners inspired her to learn more about the program and the life-changing results it creates. For Tabatha, becoming a dog trainer was driven by a genuine love for dogs and a desire to make a difference. She believes every dog deserves the opportunity to succeed and is passionate about helping families overcome behavioral challenges before they lead to difficult decisions.

Her mission is to help keep dogs in loving homes by providing owners with the knowledge, confidence, and training they need to build happy, well-balanced relationships with their companions. One of the most rewarding aspects of training for Tabatha is witnessing the transformation that occurs when dogs and their owners begin to truly understand one another. She finds great fulfillment in helping people experience the confidence and freedom that come from having a well-trained dog, while giving dogs the opportunity to thrive as valued members of the family.

Outside of dog training, Tabatha enjoys spending time outdoors. Whether she's hiking scenic trails, relaxing at the beach, or exploring Florida's beautiful freshwater springs, she appreciates every opportunity to enjoy nature and stay active. Her enthusiasm, compassion, and commitment to helping both dogs and their owners make her a valuable member of the Lorenzo's Dog Training Team.
