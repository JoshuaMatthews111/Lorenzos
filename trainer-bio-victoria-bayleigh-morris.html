About Victoria Bayleigh Morris

Victoria Bayleigh Morris Victoria Bayleigh Morris was born in Ohio and currently resides in Milton, Florida. Her love of animals brought her to become a veterinary technician at a vet clinic, where she met one of LDTT’s trainers during a Lunch and Learn. While watching the trainer work their dog, she saw what a well-trained dog could really do, and she discovered her own dog’s capabilities in the attention and respect he demonstrated.

Her thoughts then turned to wanting that for herself. She contacted LDTT and was one of the first to experience our new learning platform of online training. Her goals are for her dog, Neagan, to be her “right hand man” and to be able to work for herself.

In her time away from training, Bayleigh likes to ride her motorcycle and spend time on the water.
