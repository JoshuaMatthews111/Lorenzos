Specialty services are designed for serious clients, businesses, residential homes, kennel owners, doggy day care operators, service animal needs, and approved handlers.

Protection Training

Advanced protection dog training for businesses, residential homes, kennel owners, doggy day care operators, and approved handlers. The phrase suspicion level building means teaching a dog to notice unusual behavior and respond with awareness under handler direction, not uncontrolled aggression.

Retrieval Training

Object retrieval training teaches the dog to pick up, hold, carry, and release items of different shapes, weights, and materials on command.

Scent Work Training

Scent work uses the dog's natural ability to locate objects, follow scent, and work with focus through structured search tasks.

Utility Training

Utility training develops advanced obstacle work, balance, confidence, distance control, and reliable handling in demanding environments.

Service Dog Training

Task-based service dog training supports owners with physical disabilities or handicap-related needs, with practical routines built around the handler's daily life.

Assistance Dog Training

Assistance dog training supports specialized daily-life tasks, owner confidence, reliable control, and a dog that can work calmly in real environments.
