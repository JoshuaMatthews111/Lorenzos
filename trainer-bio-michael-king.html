About Michael King

Michael King Navarre, Florida In his youth, Michael King moved around a bit, going from California to Arizona, then finally landing in Navarre, Florida, where he presently resides. In his life, he’s informally trained many dogs to do basic commands and learned how to work with people while in the service industry. He also served in the Air Force! Michael found out about Lorenzo’s when a friend referred him to an LDTT trainer, who helped him with his aggressive dog, Odin.

When asked why he wanted to become a dog trainer, he said, “…because it would give me the opportunity to run a business, as well as be able to save and help as many dogs as I can. There’s nothing more that I want than to be able to run a business, play with dogs, and love doing it all at the same time.” When Michael isn’t training dogs, he enjoys playing guitar, video games, hiking, kayaking, fishing, and camping.
